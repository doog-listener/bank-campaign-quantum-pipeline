Five stages, one frozen protocol

Every learned statistic is fitted inside a fold. The selection rule was hashed before any model was trained.

Stage 0–1

Classical baseline. 5×10 repeated stratified CV, 3 registered candidates, nested sigmoid calibration.

ROC-AUC 0.6463

Stage 2

Quantum classifiers. ZZ fidelity kernel, data-reuploading VQC, plus the ablations that make the comparison fair.

gate: rejected

Stage 3–4

Selection. One probability vector, top 10, 20-qubit QUBO, brute force, standard + Dicke/XY QAOA.

optimum 39.7849

Stage 5–6

Outcome. 6-qubit circuit, exact Poisson-binomial, shot/noise/mitigation runs, traceability bundle.

The protocol is the deliverable. Test rows are never used for features, tuning, calibration, thresholds or model selection; the admission rule and its fingerprint were written into PREREGISTRATION.md before Stage 1 ran, and Stage 3 refuses to continue if the artifact it reads was produced under a different fingerprint.

Stage 4 — an exact answer, and constraints that never bite

QUBO

min −Σ wᵢxᵢ + 40(Σx−6)² + 12(Σc·x+s_b−25)² + 12(Σh·x+s_c−22)²

quantityboundworst case over all 210 subsets

Findings

  • All 210 subsets are feasible, so the QUBO's feasible set is the whole weight-6 shell and the optimum is simply the six largest wᵢ.
  • The budget and capacity rows cannot change the answer for this instance.
  • QUBO ground state (−39.7849) equals the brute-force optimum; the run aborts if it does not.

Stated plainly: on this instance no optimiser — quantum or classical — can beat sorting six numbers. The pipeline says so instead of dressing it up.

What the numbers say

  • Soft penalties spend amplitude on low-cardinality states; more depth does not fix it (energy 2008 → 2554 vs ground −39.8).
  • Dicke/XY holds Σx=6 exactly, and by p=4 its single most probable bitstring is the optimum: 838/8 192 shots at p=5.

Simulators verified against Qiskit/Aer on the full problems: max |ΔP| = 2.6e-16 (standard), 4.5e-16 (Dicke).

Model uncertainty is ~40× quantum shot noise. The frozen-selection mean sits below the point estimate because refitting shrinks extreme probabilities.

Selection stability

Under bootstrap, the top-10 candidate set is identical in 2.5 % of re-solves, and the six-customer optimum repeats in 0.5 %.

Mean regret of the frozen pick: 10.1 of 39.78 (p95 32.8).

This is the project's main negative result: the decision is dominated by 300 rows of training variance, not by any quantum effect.

What we claim, and what we do not

Claimed

  • A reproducible pipeline: 144 tests, seeded, one-command reproduce.
  • An exact, verified answer: 6 customers, 39.7849 net value, 0.567655 success probability.
  • Quantified decomposition of classical vs quantum contributions — including where quantum lost.

Not claimed

  • No quantum advantage. The Stage 4 instance is solved exactly by 210-way enumeration and by sorting.
  • No hardware results: simulators only (qiskit-aer 0.18.2).
  • No test-set accuracy: the release contains no test labels.
